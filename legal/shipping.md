# Shipping

Every piece is sent from Amsterdam with PostNL, as a tracked parcel. We ship worldwide, except to {{notShipped}}.

Your order leaves within {{shipWithin}} after your payment. As soon as it is on its way you receive the track & trace
code by email.

## Costs

Up to {{perParcel}} pieces travel in one parcel. A larger order is sent in several parcels and their costs are added
up. The cart shows the exact shipping costs for your country before you pay.

{{shippingTable}}

## Delivery times

Counted from the day your parcel leaves, as an indication: the Netherlands and Belgium 1–3 working days, the rest of
Europe 3–8 working days, other countries 5–15 working days.

## Customs

Within the European Union there are no customs formalities. Outside the EU your country's customs or the delivery
service may charge import VAT or duties on delivery; those are for the buyer.
**United States:** the import duties and fees are included in the shipping costs. You pay nothing on delivery.

## Lost or damaged

If your parcel has not arrived after the delivery time above, or arrives damaged, write to us at {{email}} and we will
sort it out with PostNL.
