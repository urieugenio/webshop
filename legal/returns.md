# Returns

You may return a piece within 14 days without giving a reason. This is your right of withdrawal under European
consumer law.

## How to return a piece

1. Tell us within **14 days after you received your order** that you want to return it: write to {{email}}, or send
   us the form below. Mention your name and which piece or pieces you return.
2. Send the piece back **within 14 days after telling us**, to:
   {{business}}, {{address}}.
3. Pack it well and keep the proof of postage. The **return shipping costs are for you**.

## Your refund

We refund the price you paid for the returned pieces, together with the shipping costs of your order (for the
standard shipping we offer), within 14 days after you told us you are returning it. We may wait until we have received
the piece back. You get the money on the payment method you paid with, at no cost to you.

You may try a piece on, as you would in a shop. A piece that has been worn, washed, damaged or that smells of smoke or
perfume loses value; we may deduct that loss from the refund.

## Something wrong with your piece?

Every piece is made by hand, so small irregularities in the stitches and colours are part of it and are not a defect.
If your piece is damaged or not as described, write to us at {{email}} with a photo. Your rights under the legal
guarantee always apply, also after the 14 days.
